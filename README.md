## AI Policy
The vast majority of our code is reviewed by AI for obvious security/performance flaws. Some of us use AI assistants to write code, while other's don't. While others may disagree, we find the difference in quality marginal enough to not care about (our own) AI PRs. Many of our PRs are AI generated as some of us are ESL. With that said, all of our code is read by at least one human, most of it read by at least a few of us.
